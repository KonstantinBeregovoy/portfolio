### Solving the Performance Mystery

On the technical side, the standout session for me was <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/opherv/" target="_blank"><strong>Opher Vishnia</strong></a>**’s** talk: *"Quick Wins to Improve Your App’s Performance."*

![TODO: image description](https://media.licdn.com/dms/image/v2/D4D12AQFjRqvigwb2iw/article-inline_image-shrink_1500_2232/B4DZxnJqXMIoAU-/0/1771257116291?e=1793232000&v=beta&t=tAFwczxL-6TXf-KcJNs6SQSFsBcMeHWT0IWtxRs4PiU)

Opher turned the session into a noir detective story, where we hunted down the "culprits" sabotaging our apps' frame rates. **Key takeaways we’ve already started applying:**

- **Context as a "Footgun":** Being much more careful with unstable references in useContext to avoid unnecessary tree re-renders.

- **Derived State vs. Effects:** Eliminating redundant useEffect hooks where data can be computed during render.

- **Intentional Memoization:** Applying React.memo as a precision tool, rather than a "silver bullet," to ensure the cost of memoization doesn't outweigh the benefits.

Two months in, I can see how these shifts are already making our products faster and our codebase cleaner. Huge thanks to my team for the company and to all the speakers for the inspiration!
